📖 What Is an Equation?

An equation is a mathematical statement that says two things are equal.

For example:

5 + 3 = 8

The left side is 8.

The right side is 8.

So they are equal.

Think of an equation like a balance scale.

⚖️

If both sides have the same value, the scale is balanced.

🔍 What About an Unknown?

Consider:

x + 5 = 12

We don't know what x is.

Our job is to find it.

Ask yourself:

What number plus 5 equals 12?

The answer is:

7

Therefore:

x = 7

🧠 Check Your Answer

Always put your answer back into the original equation.

x + 5 = 12

Replace x with 7:

7 + 5 = 12

Correct! ✅

✏️ Practice

⭐ Remember

An equation is like a balance. Whatever you do to one side, you must do to the other side.

📘 DAY 12 — Solving Addition Equations
🎯 Today's Goal

Learn how to solve equations like:

x + 6 = 14

We need to get x by itself.

🔄 Use the Opposite Operation

The equation says:

x + 6 = 14

We want to remove +6.

The opposite of adding 6 is subtracting 6.

So:

x + 6 - 6 = 14 - 6

The 6 disappears:

x = 8

🧠 Why Does This Work?

Because we performed the same operation on both sides.

Think of a balance:

⚖️

If you remove 6 from the left side, you must remove 6 from the right side.

The balance stays equal.

Example

x + 9 = 20

Subtract 9 from both sides:

x = 11

Check:

11 + 9 = 20 ✅

📘 DAY 13 — Solving Subtraction Equations
🎯 Today's Goal

Solve equations like:

x - 5 = 12

🔄 Think About the Opposite

We have:

x - 5 = 12

The opposite of subtracting 5 is adding 5.

So:

x - 5 + 5 = 12 + 5

Therefore:

x = 17

Check:

17 - 5 = 12 ✅

Example

x - 8 = 15

Add 8 to both sides:

x = 23

Check:

23 - 8 = 15 ✅

📘 DAY 14 — Multiplication Equations
🎯 Today's Goal

Solve equations like:

3x = 18

🔍 What Does 3x Mean?

Remember:

3x means 3 × x

So:

3 × x = 18

We need to find x.

The opposite of multiplication is division.

Divide both sides by 3:

3x ÷ 3 = 18 ÷ 3

Therefore:

x = 6

Example

5x = 25

Divide both sides by 5:

x = 5

Check:

5 × 5 = 25 ✅

✏️ Practice

📘 DAY 15 — Division Equations
🎯 Today's Goal

Solve equations like:

x ÷ 4 = 5

🔄 Use the Opposite Operation

The equation says:

x ÷ 4 = 5

The opposite of division is multiplication.

Multiply both sides by 4:

x ÷ 4 × 4 = 5 × 4

Therefore:

x = 20

Check:

20 ÷ 4 = 5 ✅

Example

x ÷ 6 = 7

Multiply both sides by 6:

x = 42

✏️ Practice

To undo division, multiply.

📘 DAY 16 — Mixed One-Step Equations

Now let's put everything together.

Remember the four rules:

Equation	What to do
x + 5 = 12	Subtract 5
x - 5 = 12	Add 5
5x = 20	Divide by 5
x ÷ 5 = 4	Multiply by 5
🧠 Your Strategy

When you see an equation:

Step 1

Look at what is being done to x.

Step 2

Use the opposite operation.

Step 3

Do it to both sides.

Step 4

Check your answer.

🏆 SECTION 4 SUCCESS CHECK

Before starting Section 5, the student should be able to explain:

"When solving an equation, I use the opposite operation to get the variable by itself."

He should know:

Addition ↔ Subtraction

Multiplication ↔ Division

And most importantly:

Whatever I do to one side of an equation, I must do to the other side.

Once this is comfortable, Days 18–24 will move to Two-Step Equations, where he will learn problems such as:

3x + 5 = 20

and eventually:

4(x − 3) + 7 = 31.
