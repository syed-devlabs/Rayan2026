# Math assignment
📖 What is Algebra?

Algebra is a way of using numbers, letters, and symbols to solve problems.

You already use algebra in everyday life.

Imagine your friend says:

"I have some money. I don't know exactly how much. If I get $5 more, I will have $20."

We don't know how much money your friend has.

We can use a letter to represent the unknown amount.

Let:

x = the amount of money

Then:

x + 5 = 20

That's algebra!

🔤 What is a Variable?

A variable is a letter or symbol that represents a number.

Common variables are:

x, y, a, b, n

For example:

x = 7

Here, x represents the number 7.

But a variable doesn't always have to be 7.

It could be:

x = 5

or

x = 20

or

x = 100

That's why we call it a variable — its value can change.

🔢 What is a Constant?

A constant is a number that does not have a variable attached to it.

Examples:

5

12

100

In:

x + 7

x is the variable.

7 is the constant.

🧩 What is an Expression?

An expression is a combination of numbers, variables, and mathematical operations.

Examples:

x + 5

2x

3x + 7

10 - y

Notice that an expression does not have an equals sign.

Remember:

Expression: x + 5

Equation: x + 5 = 12

🟢 DAY 2 — Understanding Variables
🎯 Today's Goal

Learn how to find the value of an expression when you know the value of the variable.

📖 What Does "Evaluate" Mean?

In algebra, evaluate means:

Find the value of an expression.

Suppose:

x = 5

What is:

x + 3?

Replace x with 5:

5 + 3 = 8

Therefore:

x + 3 = 8

🔄 Substitution

Replacing a variable with its value is called substitution.

Example:

x = 6

Find:

x + 4

Replace x:

6 + 4 = 10

Answer:

10

Another Example

If:

x = 5

Find:

2x

2x means:

2 × x

So:

2 × 5 = 10

Therefore:

2x = 10

🟢 DAY 3 — Terms and Coefficients
🎯 Today's Goal

Learn to identify the different parts of an algebra expression.

Consider:

4x + 7

There are two parts:

4x and 7

These parts are called terms.

🔹 What is a Term?

A term can be:

A number: 5
A variable: x
A number multiplied by a variable: 4x

Examples:

3x + 5

Terms are:

3x and 5

🔢 What is a Coefficient?

The number directly multiplying a variable is called the coefficient.

In:

5x

5 is the coefficient.

In:

9y

9 is the coefficient.

In:

x

the coefficient is actually 1.

So:

x = 1x

Example

Look at:

7x + 12

7 → coefficient
x → variable
12 → constant
7x and 12 → terms

🟢 DAY 6 — Order of Operations
🎯 Today's Goal

Learn the correct order for solving mathematical expressions.

You may know this as BEDMAS.

BEDMAS

B → Brackets
E → Exponents
D → Division
M → Multiplication
A → Addition
S → Subtraction

Example

Solve:

3 + 2 × 5

Do NOT add first.

Multiplication comes before addition.

First:

2 × 5 = 10

Then:

3 + 10 = 13

Answer:

13

Example With Brackets

(3 + 2) × 5

Brackets come first:

3 + 2 = 5

Then:

5 × 5 = 25

Answer:

25

Notice:

3 + 2 × 5 = 13

but

(3 + 2) × 5 = 25

The brackets changed the answer.

✏️ Practice

5 + 2 × 6
(5 + 2) × 6
10 - 3 × 2
(10 - 3) × 2
4 + 3 × 5
(4 + 3) × 5
⭐ Remember

Don't automatically calculate from left to right. Follow BEDMAS.

🟢 DAY 8 — Like Terms
🎯 Today's Goal

Understand which algebra terms can be combined.

What Are Like Terms?

Like terms have the same variable.

Examples:

3x and 5x

These are like terms.

So:

3x + 5x = 8x

Another Example

7x - 2x

Take away 2 from 7:

5x

Therefore:

7x - 2x = 5x

🚫 These Are NOT Like Terms

3x and 5

You cannot combine them.

Why?

Because 3x contains a variable and 5 does not.

So:

3x + 5

stays:

3x + 5

Think About Objects

Imagine:

🍎 + 🍎 + 🍎 = 3 apples

You can combine apples with apples.

But:

🍎 + 🚗

cannot become "2 something."

Algebra works similarly.

3x + 5x = 8x

But:

3x + 5

cannot be combined.

🟢 DAY 9 — Simplifying Expressions
🎯 Today's Goal

Learn how to make a complicated expression simpler.

Example

Simplify:

4x + 3 + 2x + 5

First find the like terms.

Variable terms:

4x + 2x

Constants:

3 + 5

Now combine them:

4x + 2x = 6x

3 + 5 = 8

Therefore:

6x + 8

Another Example

Simplify:

7x + 4 - 3x + 2

Combine x terms:

7x - 3x = 4x

Combine constants:

4 + 2 = 6

Answer:

4x + 6

A Useful Strategy

When simplifying:

Step 1

Find all the variable terms.

Step 2

Find all the constants.

Step 3

Combine like terms.

Step 4

Write the simplified answer.
